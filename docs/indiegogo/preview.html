Veterans shouldn't have to reach a breaking point before support shows up.

Every veteran crisis has a before. Lost sleep. Skipped meals. Calls that go unanswered. Benefits paperwork that piles up. The warning signs are visible — but they're scattered across family, buddies, and case workers who each see only a piece, until someone is in crisis.

SUAS Veteran Crisis Q.R.F. is building the infrastructure that connects those pieces earlier — so support arrives while it can still be light.

A Quick Reaction Force for support

Risks & challenges

Honest about where we are

This is early-stage: the platform is a working demo, and the pilot is where we prove workflows. Recruiting veterans takes trust, so we work through partner organizations. Privacy is hard, which is why legal review is funded before launch — and we make no clinical or outcome claims a pilot hasn't earned. Every backer gets the findings report, win or lose.
