## 1. What the top campaigns teach us (distilled)

Analysis of the highest-grossing and most-studied campaigns — Pebble Time
($20M+), Coolest Cooler ($13M+), Reading Rainbow ($6M+), Veronica Mars ($5.7M),
Wish I Was Here ($3.1M), Flow Hive, Solar Roadways, An Hour of Code, The Bounce
Back, and BauBax — plus Indiegogo's education center:

| # | Element | Evidence / rule of thumb | How SUAS applies it |
|---|---------|--------------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Pitch video first** | Campaigns with video raise ~4× more; ideal 60–90 s; first 30 s decides | 75-second script below; Jacob on camera in the first 10 s |
| 2 | **Hook in one sentence** | Reading Rainbow / Veronica Mars led with a single emotional promise | "Reach veterans before the crisis — not after." |
| 3 | **Real people, real faces** | Backers fund humans, not logos (Zach Braff, LeVar Burton effect) | Founder story + team photos; veteran-led framing |
| 4 | **Solve a named problem** | Every mega-campaign names one concrete pain | Warning signs stay disconnected until someone is in crisis |
| 5 | **Goal = true minimum** | Fast-funding campaigns get algorithm boost; 30% in 48 h predicts success | Modest goal + stretch goals, warm list primed for day 1 |
| 6 | **5–8 perks, $25 sweet spot** | $25 is the most-purchased perk across the platform | 7 tiers below, anchored at $25 |
| 7 | **Impact-framed perks** | Nonprofit campaigns convert best when a dollar = a concrete outcome | "$50 = one veteran onboarded and supported for a month of pilot check-ins" |
| 8 | **Scannable story with images** | Break text every 2–3 paragraphs; show perks visually | Section structure + image checklist below |
| 9 | **Community before cash** | Reading Rainbow & Bounce Back grew from partnerships, not ads | Veteran orgs, county partners, VSO chapters as launch amplifiers |
| 10 | **Update cadence** | Successful campaigns post 1–2 updates/week; updates re-trigger backer shares | Update calendar in §8 |

Nonprofit-specific additions: Indiegogo allows **flexible funding** (keep what
you raise) — right for a nonprofit where any amount funds real work; the
**secret perk** feature rewards your email list and partner orgs; and
recognition/experience perks preserve tax-deductibility better than merch.

## 4. Campaign story (paste into the Story editor)

> Image checklist woven in — replace `[IMG:]` markers with real photos. Never
> use stock photos pretending to be pilot veterans.

### Opening hook

**Veterans shouldn't have to reach a breaking point before support shows up.**

Every veteran crisis has a before. Lost sleep. Skipped meals. Calls that go
unanswered. Benefits paperwork that piles up. The warning signs are visible —
but they're scattered across family members, buddies, and case workers who each
see only a piece, until someone is in crisis.

**SUAS Veteran Crisis Q.R.F. is building the infrastructure that connects those
pieces earlier** — so support arrives while it can still be light.

`[IMG: hero photo — founder or veterans, warm, human]`

> 🇺🇸 **If you or a veteran you love needs help right now:** Veterans Crisis
> Line — **dial 988 and press 1**, or **text 838255**. Free, confidential, 24/7.

### The problem

- **Veterans** often carry stress privately — nightmares, withdrawal, avoiding calls.
- **Families** see pieces, not the full picture — short answers, missed dinners, irritability.
- **Nonprofits and responders** lose track between touchpoints — outreach goes cold, referrals stall.

Nobody connects the dots until the dots become an emergency.

### Our solution: a Quick Reaction Force for support

`[IMG: annotated app demo screenshots — check-in, support signal, trusted circle]`

1. **Check in** — a private, judgment-free daily check-in: mood, sleep, safety.
2. **Support signal** — check-ins roll up to a simple signal: stable → light support → active outreach.
3. **Trusted circle** — consent-based alerts to the family, peers, and responders the *veteran* chooses. Nothing is shared without consent.
4. **Resource routing** — county-relevant help for housing, benefits, and health.
5. **Follow-up** — responders log outreach so no one falls through the cracks.

**What SUAS is not:** not a diagnosis tool, not surveillance, not a replacement
for emergency care. It's early-support coordination, privacy-first by design.

## 5. Perk tiers (7 tiers + 1 secret)

Designed for the nonprofit sweet spots ($25 most-purchased platform-wide),
impact-framed, and recognition/experience-heavy so gifts stay largely
tax-deductible (⚠️ have the tax preparer confirm quid-pro-quo treatment for any
physical item; state fair-market value on perks that include goods).

| Price | Name | What backers get |
|---|---|---|
| $10 | **Fire Watch** | Name on the digital Founding Supporters wall at suasqrf.org + backer-only updates |
| $25 | **Battle Buddy** ⭐ anchor | Fire Watch + founding-supporter digital certificate + pilot findings report |
| $50 | **Overwatch** | Battle Buddy + *impact: supports one veteran through a month of pilot check-ins* |
| $100 | **Squad Leader** | Overwatch + SUAS challenge coin ⚠️(or sticker pack if coin cost too high) |
| $250 | **Platoon Sponsor** | Squad Leader + name/logo on the pilot findings report + live virtual pilot briefing with the founder |
| $500 | **Company Sponsor** | Platoon Sponsor + *impact: funds a full pilot seat — one veteran, onboarding through findings* |
| $1,000 | **Mission Partner** | Company Sponsor + recognition on the website partners section + 1:1 briefing call |

day-1 urgency; every perk image shows the *impact*, not just the item; no perk
requiring manufacturing lead time you can't meet.

## 6. FAQ (paste into campaign FAQ)

- **Is my contribution tax-deductible?** Yes — SUAS is a 501(c)(3) (EIN 88-3249428), deductible to the extent allowed by law. Perks with physical goods may reduce the deductible portion by their fair-market value.
- **Is SUAS a crisis hotline?** No. In an emergency, call 911 or the Veterans Crisis Line (988, press 1 / text 838255). SUAS is early-support coordination *before* crisis.
- **Who sees a veteran's check-ins?** Only the trusted circle the veteran chooses, by explicit consent. Privacy-first is a design requirement, funded before launch.
- **What if you raise more / less than the goal?** More: stretch goals (second county, language access). Less (flexible funding): we scale the pilot cohort to funds raised and tell you exactly how.
- **When does the pilot start?** ⚠️ timeline answer from §4.
- **Can my organization partner?** Yes — nonprofits, VSOs, and county programs: contact us via suasqrf.org/contact.

## 7. Pre-launch checklist (the 30%-in-48-hours play)

The single strongest predictor of success is a fast start. Before launch:

1. **Build the warm list (3–4 weeks out):** pre-launch landing email capture, personal outreach to every past donor, board members each commit to bring 5 backers day 1.
2. **Line up partners:** 3–5 veteran orgs / VSO chapters agree to share on launch day; draft their posts *for* them.
3. **Assets ready:** video, 10+ story images, perk graphics, 5 social cut-downs (15 s vertical), press one-pager.
4. **Day-1 commitments:** aim for pledges covering ~30% of goal before you press launch. If you can't see 30%, lower the goal or delay.
5. **Local press:** pitch the human story (veteran-founded, county pilot) to local TV/papers the week of launch — community campaigns win local media in ways gadgets can't.

## 8. During-campaign cadence

- **Day 1:** launch email + partner posts + personal texts. Thank every backer by name within hours.
- **2× weekly updates:** milestone hit, team intro, demo walkthrough, backer shout-outs, stretch-goal reveal. Every update = a new share moment.
- **Mid-campaign slump (days 10–20):** release the secret perk to the email list; publish one substantive post (e.g., "How consent-based alerts actually work").
- **Final 72 hours:** urgency emails ("48 hours left"), partner re-shares, last stretch push. Final-3-days typically = ~20–30% of total raise.
- **After close:** thank-you update within 24 h, then monthly pilot updates until the findings report ships. Backers are your future donor base — Indiegogo is the top of the funnel, not the end.
