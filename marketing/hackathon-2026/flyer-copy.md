## Design notes (match site brand)

- Palette: navy `#1f2f46`-family background, cream text, gold accent for the
  headline and CTA (mirror `--navy`, `--cream`, `--gold` from the website).
- The crisis-line strip is non-negotiable on every printed piece — high
  contrast, never smaller than the registration CTA.
- QR code points to <https://luma.com/Innovation4Veterans>.
- Photography: silhouettes/hands-on-keyboards; no identifiable veterans
  without written consent.
