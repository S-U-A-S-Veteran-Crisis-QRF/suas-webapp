## Bay Area Veterans and Technologists to Build Crisis-Response Platform at Three-Day Hackathon in Mountain View

**Veterans Innovation Hackathon 2026 runs August 28–30 at Hacker Dojo; public Demo Day showcases working tools that connect veterans in crisis to food, transportation, and same-night shelter**

MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif. — August [XX], 2026 — S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF, a
Santa Clara County 501(c)(3) veteran crisis nonprofit, will host the
**Veterans Innovation Hackathon 2026** on **August 28–30 at Hacker Dojo (855
Maude Ave, Mountain View)**, bringing together veterans, engineers, designers,
and mentors to build technology that answers the three things veterans in
crisis most often ask for: a meal, a ride to care, and a safe place to sleep
tonight.

Over three days, teams will work across five challenge tracks — food
fulfillment, crisis transportation, emergency shelter, security and HIPAA
compliance, and veteran user experience — to advance the organization's
"Food · Ride · Shelter" crisis app from working prototype toward a
production platform. The event culminates in a **public Demo Day on Sunday,
August 30**, judged by veterans, technologists, and community leaders.

"Veterans don't attend hackathons — veterans attend missions," said **Jacob
Silver, founder and executive director of S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF**.
"This weekend is a mission: build the tool veterans asked for, with veterans
in the room shaping every screen."

Unlike a typical hackathon, participation does not require a technical
background. Veterans attend as problem-owners whose experience drives what
gets built; engineers and designers supply the hands. Teams form on site,
all skill levels are welcome, and attendance is free.

The winning work will not end with the weekend: the strongest teams will be
invited to continue with the organization into a supervised pilot serving
Santa Clara County veterans.

**Event details**

- **What:** Veterans Innovation Hackathon 2026
- **When:** Friday, August 28, 5:00 PM – Sunday, August 30, 9:00 PM; public Demo Day Sunday from 11:00 AM
- **Where:** Hacker Dojo, 855 Maude Ave, Mountain View, CA 94043
- **Cost:** Free; registration required at <https://luma.com/Innovation4Veterans>
- **Who:** Veterans (tech and non-tech), engineers, designers, product managers, mentors, and judges

**About S.U.A.S. Veteran Crisis QRF**

S.U.A.S. ("Shut Up And Serve") Veteran Crisis QRF is a California 501(c)(3)
public charity (EIN 88-3249428) based in Los Altos, providing peer-bridge
crisis support that connects veterans in crisis to help, fast. Learn more at
suasqrf.org.

*If you are a veteran in crisis or concerned about one, contact the Veterans
Crisis Line: dial 988 and press 1, or text 838255. S.U.A.S. is not emergency
care.*
